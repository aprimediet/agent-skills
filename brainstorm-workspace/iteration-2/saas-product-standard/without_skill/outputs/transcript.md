# Transcript — Without-Skill Brainstorm

**Task**: Brainstorm a SaaS for small dental clinics (appointment management, reminders, intake forms) targeting solo practitioners and small clinics with 1–5 dentists.

**Process**:

1. **Reviewed the task** — understood the target audience (solo practitioners / small clinics), core features (appointments, reminders, intake forms), and scope.

2. **Structured the brainstorm** — decided to cover 10 dimensions: market context, user personas, feature roadmap (MVP → growth → scale), technical architecture, business model, risks, open questions, MVP scope, success criteria, and next steps.

3. **Drafted the document** — wrote to `saas-product-standard/without_skill/outputs/dental-saas-brainstorm.md`.

4. **Key thinking**:
   - Kept the focus narrow on the 1–5 dentist segment — larger practices have different needs.
   - Designed the feature tiers so the MVP is shippable in 8–10 weeks with a small team.
   - Added specific compliance (HIPAA) considerations since dental data is protected health information.
   - Included concrete pricing tiers and go-to-market channels to ground the brainstorming in reality.
   - Flagged open questions explicitly — brainstorming should surface unknowns, not hide them.
   - Made explicit trade-off calls in the MVP section (what to exclude is as important as what to include).

5. **No external context was used** — this is entirely based on general knowledge of SaaS patterns, dental practice operations, and healthcare technology constraints.
